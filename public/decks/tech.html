How cercit is built.

A credit appraisal system for salaried new-car loans: documents read automatically, two bureaus, versioned policy rules, a risk model, and a person who signs off.

100database tables
460database functions
9AWS Lambda services
87migrations, each tested and logged

The principle

AI assists. Policy decides. A person signs off.

Four parts, kept apart on purpose, so each can be checked on its own.

1 · Document AI

What does the document say?

Reads PAN, Aadhaar, payslips, Form 16 and bank statements, and matches them against each other.

2 · Data engine

How does this person handle money?

Bureau history, income from three sources, EMIs already running, bank behaviour.

3 · Risk model

How likely is a late payment?

XGBoost, trained on synthetic loans, gives a grade and a probability.

4 · Policy engine

Does it qualify under our rules?

Deterministic, versioned, auditable. The model informs; the rules decide.

Life of an application

From a phone upload to a decision.

02Check and maskReal PDF/JPG/PNG under 10 MB, or deleted. Aadhaar digits and QR blacked out.document_finalize · zxing-cpp
03ReadTextract plus cercit's own readers for each document type.kyc · salary_slip · form16 · bank
04Cross-checkName, birth date, address, face, payslip age. Rules are rows in a table.cross_validator · document_check_rules

06IncomeDeclared, payslip and bank salary compared; the lowest counts.income assessment

08Sign off and payOfficer decides. Offer, e-sign, e-mandate, dealer paid, repayments tracked.loan_accounts · schedules

Policy engine

Rules are data, and no one approves their own change.

DraftThe Credit Head edits a copy: switch a rule, change a limit, a rate, a role.
→
Impact testThe draft is run against recent cases (Zen rules engine on Lambda) to show which decisions would flip.
→
ApproveOnly the Admin approves. The database refuses an approval by the author.
→
Live on a dateThe version starts on its date; the old one is kept as history.

Every decision is traceable

It stores the policy version, a fingerprint of the rules it used, and the model version that scored it.

Conflicting rights blocked

5 pairs of rights can't sit on one role (e.g. decide cases and manage users) unless a waiver says why.

Same for rates and roles

The rate grid, risk models and role changes all go draft → approve → live.

Risk model

A risk score that runs on the visitor's own device.

What it is

  • XGBoost classifier, exported to ONNX
  • Predicts the chance of going 30+ days late
  • 18 inputs: bureau accounts, bank months, FOIR and more
  • Version v2 is live; v1 kept for older decisions

How it was trained

  • 16,995 synthetic customers, published as training data
  • The live inputs are worked out the same way as the training data, and a test checks it case by case
  • Gives a grade (A to E) and a probability

It informs the decision. It never overrides a policy rule.

Security and privacy

Built as if the data were real.

Access

Least privilege

8 roles, 32 rights. Row-level security on every table. Pages read through 204 checked database functions, never straight from tables.

Personal data

Masked and encrypted

PAN and mobile encrypted at rest, shown masked. Aadhaar masked before it is stored. Full PAN only with a logged "reveal".

Location

Data stays in India

Database, documents, readers and face match all run in Mumbai (ap-south-1).

DPDP Act

Erase on request

Personal data can be erased on request, except where KYC rules require keeping it for 5 years. Every file version is deleted too.

Audit

Who did what, when

Every decision, override, rule change and sign-in is written to the audit log by the database itself.

Sign-in

Locks and timeouts

Lockout after failed tries, idle timeout per role, and two-step sign-in ready for the Admin and Head (off in this demo).

Running it

A demo that keeps moving on its own.

Every morning, 06:00 IST

A scheduled database job (pg_cron) adds 10 synthetic applications, pays out approved loans, and makes the day's repayments, including some that bounce or go late. The loan book and PAR figures stay current.

Every Sunday

A full copy of every settings, policy and pricing table is saved. No personal data is in it.

Try it as the team

Visitors sign in as Head, Manager or Officer with one click. Anything they change outside a case is journaled, and the Admin undoes it with one button.

Feedback on the way out

Signing out asks how it went, where people got lost, and what broke, with contact details only with consent.
