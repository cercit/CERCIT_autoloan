cercit · Investor brief

Credit appraisal that writes itself.

AI drafts the credit appraisal memo for new vehicle loans. The lender's own policy makes the decision.

Early-stage product · Demo on synthetic data · September 2026

The problem

Underwriting is the slow step

25–40

credit appraisal memos an officer builds by hand, every working day

Re-keying

Bureau reports, bank statements and salary slips typed into the memo by hand

Inconsistency

Two officers can read the same file two different ways

Audit load

Reasons and deviations are pieced together after the fact

cercit · Investor brief · 02 · Workload figure from the founder's years in vehicle finance

What cercit is

An AI co-pilot that drafts the memo, not the decision

Document AI

Reads PAN, salary slips, Form 16 and bank statements, and cross-checks them

Data engine

Turns bureau and bank data into income, FOIR and repayment behaviour

Risk model

Scores default risk from past patterns and shows the reasons

Policy engine

Runs the lender's own versioned rules. The decision is made here.

AI assists. Policy decides. People handle the exceptions.

How it decides

01

Hard filters

02

Bureau

03

Income

FOIR, surplus, salary pattern

04

Collateral

LTV on a known car price

05

Risk model

Default probability, explained

06

Policy verdict

Approve, review or decline
