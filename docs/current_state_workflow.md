# How a salaried new-car loan gets appraised today

Manual process as it actually works at banks and NBFCs in India. Compiled from practitioner accounts across Sundaram Finance, IndusInd Bank, Mahindra Finance, Cholamandalam, SBI, and HDFC Bank — field-level and credit-desk-level.

Scope: salaried customers, new cars only.

---

## The people involved

| Role | What they do | Where they sit |
|---|---|---|
| Sales executive / RM | Sources the lead, collects documents, builds the file, follows up with customer and dealer | Dealer showroom or field |
| Field investigation officer | Visits residence and office, verifies address, employment, living standard | On the road |
| Credit underwriter / analyst | Pulls bureau, calculates FOIR/LTV/DBR, checks policy, writes the CAM | Branch or central processing hub |
| Credit manager / sanctioning authority | Reviews the CAM, approves or rejects, sets conditions | Branch (NBFCs) or credit hub (banks) |
| Operations / disbursement desk | Checks pre-disbursement documents, triggers RTGS, tracks RC and hypothecation | Back office |

At an NBFC like Mahindra Finance or Sundaram, the branch credit manager can approve loans up to Rs 10L on the spot. At a bank like HDFC or SBI, most files go through a centralized processing centre (RACPC at SBI, credit hub at HDFC).

## Step by step

### 1. Lead sourcing (Day 0)

The customer walks into a dealership, or calls, or clicks an OEM website form. About 60-70% of salaried new-car leads come from the dealer floor. The dealer's F&I (finance and insurance) desk has codes for 4-5 lenders. Whoever gives higher funding, quicker approval, and fewer documents wins the file.

Other sources: pre-approved SMS/netbanking offers (HDFC Xpress, SBI YONO), DSA referrals, branch walk-ins (mostly SBI), direct digital applications.

The sales executive's first conversation is not a document checklist. It is file construction: which car, what variant, on-road price, quotation in hand? How much down payment? Company name, designation, take-home salary, salary-account bank, years in current job? Own house or rent? Existing EMIs? Need the car by when?

A wrong promise at this stage ("100% on-road, no Form 16 needed") becomes a cancelled booking after credit rejects the file.

**Time: 15-30 minutes for the conversation. The customer may take 1-2 days to decide and come back.**

### 2. Document collection (Day 0-2)

The sales executive collects physical or scanned documents. Standard pack for salaried:

**Identity and KYC**
- PAN card (mandatory for loans above Rs 2L)
- Aadhaar card
- One more photo ID if needed: passport, driving licence, voter ID
- 2 passport-size photographs (NBFCs still take these; digital journeys skip them)

**Address**
- Aadhaar or passport or utility bill
- Some NBFCs (Sundaram especially) still ask for own-house proof: sale deed, society bill, house-tax receipt. Banks have mostly dropped this for clean salaried profiles.

**Income**
- Last 3 months salary slips
- Form 16 (current or last financial year; SBI asks for 2 years)
- Last 6 months bank statement of salary account (IndusInd accepts 3 months; HDFC waives for existing salary-account holders)

**Employment**
- Employee ID card, or appointment letter, or HR letter on letterhead
- Official email for tele-verification

**Vehicle and deal**
- Dealer quotation with on-road price breakup
- Booking receipt
- Insurance quote

**Repayment**
- Cancelled cheque or live account proof for NACH/e-mandate setup

The sales executive checks the bank statement before filing. If salary is not credited regularly (same date, same amount, every month), the file will be rejected or approved at lower funding. A customer with Rs 60,000 salary but irregular bank flow is riskier than someone with Rs 35,000 salary and clean, regular credits.

**Time: 30-60 minutes if the customer has everything ready. Many customers take 2-5 days to bring salary slips or bank statements.**

### 3. Application login and bureau pull (Day 1-3)

The sales executive fills the loan application form (paper or tablet app like M-Fin at Mahindra, IBAP at IndusInd) and submits it with all documents to the processing centre or branch credit desk.

At login:
- CIBIL / bureau is pulled. Some banks do a soft pull at enquiry stage (HDFC, IndusInd), some do a hard pull only at formal login (SBI).
- The system generates a credit scorecard (at banks with automation) or the file enters a manual queue (at NBFCs and for non-standard profiles).
- Field investigation is triggered: residence verification and office verification.

At HDFC, pre-approved salary-account customers can get a 10-second approval and 30-minute disbursement through Xpress Car Loan. This is the exception, not the rule. Most files go through the standard process described here.

**Time: Filing takes 15-30 minutes if documents are clean. Bureau pull is instant (real-time at banks, same-day at most NBFCs).**

### 4. Field investigation (Day 2-4)

A field investigation officer physically visits:

**Residence verification:** Does the customer actually live at the declared address? Owned or rented? Neighbourhood, building quality, nameplate on door. The officer takes GPS-tagged photographs and fills a standard FI report form.

**Office verification / tele-PD:** Does the customer actually work at the declared company? The officer visits the office or the credit desk calls HR to confirm employment, designation, and tenure. For well-known employers (Category A: TCS, Infosys, government, PSUs), some banks waive physical office FI and do a phone call instead.

Sundaram Finance does the most thorough FI. Their branch managers in South India have often known the customer's family for years. This is genuine due diligence, not a formality.

Banks with digital FI (HDFC, IndusInd) use GPS-tagged photos and can complete FI faster, but the verification is thinner.

**Time: 1-2 days. This is the most common bottleneck. Rural areas and small towns take longer because the FI officer has to travel.**

### 5. Credit assessment and CAM preparation (Day 3-5)

This is where the actual underwriting happens. The credit analyst or underwriter sits down with the file and works through each check manually or semi-manually.

**What the underwriter does, in order:**

**a) Bureau analysis**
- Reads the CIBIL report line by line
- Checks score: 750+ is clean, 650-749 needs scrutiny, below 650 is usually a reject
- Counts active accounts, checks DPD history (any 60+ DPD in 24 months is a red flag)
- Counts bureau enquiries in last 90 days (more than 3-4 raises questions)
- Checks for writeoffs, settlements, suits filed
- Notes total existing EMIs from bureau (this often differs from what the customer declared)

**b) Income calculation**
- Takes the lower of: salary slip amount, bank-credit amount, Form 16 annual divided by 12
- If there is a mismatch between salary slip and bank credit, the underwriter flags it and may ask for a clarification letter from HR
- Variable pay (bonus, incentives) is typically excluded or counted at 20-50% depending on lender policy

**c) Obligation mapping**
- Lists all existing EMIs from bureau
- Compares with what the customer declared. Undisclosed loans are a flag.
- Checks if any EMIs are ending in 2-3 months (these may be excluded from FOIR calculation)
- Adds the proposed new car EMI

**d) FOIR calculation**
- FOIR = (all existing EMIs + proposed EMI) / net monthly salary
- Banks cap at 50-55%. NBFCs go up to 60-65%.
- If FOIR exceeds the limit, the underwriter either rejects, reduces the loan amount, or asks for a co-applicant

**e) LTV calculation**
- Loan amount / vehicle value
- "Vehicle value" = ex-showroom price at most banks, on-road at some NBFCs
- Standard: 80-90% for most salaried. Up to 100% ex-showroom or 90% on-road for prime profiles.

**f) Employer and stability check**
- Is the employer in Category A (MNC, government, PSU), B (listed mid-size), or C (unlisted, small)?
- Category determines rate slab, LTV limit, and whether physical FI is mandatory
- Employment tenure: 2+ years total, 1 year current is the standard. Mahindra Finance accepts 6 months.

**g) Bank statement review**
- Checks salary credit pattern (same date, same amount, every month?)
- Looks for bounced cheques or returned mandates
- Checks average monthly balance
- Looks for large cash deposits (needs explanation — could be family gift, property sale, or income inflation)
- Checks for EMI debits not disclosed by the customer

**h) Cross-document verification**
- Name on PAN must match Aadhaar must match salary slip must match bank account. Even a spelling difference creates a problem.
- Employer name on salary slip must match Form 16 must match bank salary credit narration.
- Salary amount across documents must be consistent within 5-10%.

**i) CAM writing**
- The underwriter writes the Credit Appraisal Memo. This is a structured document with:
  - Customer profile summary
  - Income and obligation summary
  - Bureau summary
  - FOIR, LTV, DBR calculations
  - FI findings
  - Risk observations (positives and concerns)
  - Recommendation: approve / reject / refer to higher authority
  - Proposed terms: rate, tenure, EMI, funding amount
- At most lenders, this is done in Excel or a word-processed template. Some have LOS (Loan Origination System) screens, but the underwriter still types most of it manually.

**Time: 2-4 hours for a clean file. Complex files (new job, lower CIBIL, self-employed spouse, mismatch in documents) take a full working day. The underwriter handles 8-15 files per day.**

### 6. Sanction (Day 3-5)

The credit manager or sanctioning authority reviews the CAM.

**For clean files (all checks pass, Category A employer, 750+ CIBIL):**
- Review takes 15-30 minutes
- Sanction is approved with standard terms
- At some banks with scorecard automation (HDFC, IndusInd), this step is near-instant for auto-approved profiles

**For borderline files:**
- Credit manager asks for additional documents, or a co-applicant, or a higher down payment
- May negotiate rate: risk-adjusted pricing for weaker profiles
- May reduce funding amount

**For reject files:**
- Clear policy breach: low CIBIL, high FOIR, employment instability, document mismatch
- Rejection logged with reason codes
- Customer informed (sometimes through the dealer, sometimes directly)

The sanction letter is generated: approved loan amount, interest rate, tenure, EMI, processing fee, conditions (if any).

**Time: Same day for clean files at NBFCs (branch-level authority). 1-2 days at banks where files go to a central credit hub.**

### 7. Pre-disbursement document check (Day 4-7)

Operations desk checks that all conditions are met before releasing money:

- Sanction letter signed by the customer
- Down payment / margin money received (receipt from dealer)
- Comprehensive motor insurance policy with lender as hypothecatee
- Tax invoice from dealer (replaces proforma invoice)
- NACH / e-mandate set up for EMI auto-debit
- Stamp duty paid (where applicable)
- All original KYC documents verified

**Time: 1-2 hours if everything is in order. Often delayed because insurance is pending or dealer hasn't issued the tax invoice yet.**

### 8. Disbursement (Day 5-10)

Loan amount is transferred to the dealer's account via RTGS or NEFT. The customer does not receive the money directly for new car loans.

The dealer confirms receipt, delivers the car, and initiates RTO registration with the lender's hypothecation (Form 34).

**Time: Same day if PDD check passes before the RTGS cutoff (usually 2-3 PM). Otherwise next working day.**

### 9. Post-disbursement (Day 7-30)

- RTO registration completed: RC issued with hypothecation in lender's name
- RC copy collected by the lender (dealer-managed, often takes 15-30 days)
- Welcome kit sent to customer
- First EMI starts 30-45 days after disbursement
- NACH/e-mandate activated

## Total turnaround time (real numbers)

| Lender type | Login to sanction | Sanction to disbursement | Total (lead to car delivery) |
|---|---|---|---|
| NBFC (Mahindra, Sundaram, Chola) | 1-3 working days | 1-2 working days | 4-7 days |
| Private bank (HDFC, IndusInd) | Same day to 2 days | Same day to 1 day | 2-5 days |
| PSU bank (SBI) | 3-5 working days | 2-3 working days | 7-15 days |
| HDFC Xpress (pre-approved, existing customer) | 10 seconds to 30 minutes | 30 minutes | Same day |

The variation is real. A government teacher with SBI salary account and CIBIL 800 can get sanctioned in 2 days at SBI but wait 7 more for disbursement paperwork. The same teacher walks into an HDFC branch and gets Xpress approval in 30 minutes if she already has an HDFC salary account. If she doesn't, standard process: 3-5 days.

## Where time gets wasted

| Bottleneck | How long it adds | Why it happens |
|---|---|---|
| Customer takes days to bring salary slips or bank statement | 2-5 days | Customer is busy, or needs to request from HR/bank |
| Field investigation in small towns | 1-3 days | FI officer covers multiple districts; scheduling delay |
| Centralized credit hub (banks) | 1-2 days | File queued behind 50+ others; underwriter capacity |
| Document mismatch (name spelling, salary amount) | 1-3 days | Customer asked to get correction letter from employer or bank |
| Insurance policy not yet issued | 1-2 days | Insurance company delay, or customer comparing quotes |
| Dealer hasn't issued tax invoice | 1 day | Dealer waits for full payment confirmation |
| NACH/e-mandate bounce or registration failure | 1-2 days | Wrong account details, bank-side NPCI delay |

## What the underwriter actually spends time on

For a salaried new-car file, the underwriter's 2-4 hour block breaks down roughly as:

| Activity | Time | What's happening |
|---|---|---|
| Read documents, check completeness | 20-30 min | Are all 9+ documents present? Any pages missing? Scans readable? |
| Bureau report analysis | 20-30 min | Score, DPD, enquiries, active accounts, writeoffs. Line by line. |
| Income calculation | 15-20 min | Cross-check salary slip vs bank credit vs Form 16. Resolve mismatches. |
| Bank statement review | 30-45 min | Scroll through 6 months of transactions. Flag bounces, cash deposits, EMI debits. Calculate average balance. |
| FOIR / LTV / DBR calculation | 10-15 min | Plug numbers into Excel or LOS screen. |
| Cross-document name and employer matching | 10-15 min | PAN vs Aadhaar vs salary slip vs bank account. Character by character for problem cases. |
| FI report review | 10-15 min | Read the field investigation report. Check photos. Flag discrepancies. |
| Write the CAM | 30-45 min | Type up the appraisal memo with all findings, calculations, and recommendation. |
| **Total** | **~2.5-3.5 hours** | For a clean file. Add 1-2 hours for anything non-standard. |

An underwriter handles 8-15 files per day. Clean salaried files take the same time as files with genuine risk, because the manual process doesn't distinguish — every field is checked the same way whether the outcome is obvious or not.

## Lender-specific notes

**Sundaram Finance:** Most conservative on LTV (80-85% on-road). Strongest field investigation. Branch manager has real underwriting authority and often knows the customer's family. 3-5 day TAT. Dominant in South India.

**IndusInd Bank:** On-road funding champion (90-95%). Fastest among banks for standard files (same-day sanction). Employer Category A/B/C pricing grid gives clear rate/eligibility rules. Tablet-based application with real-time CIBIL auto-decisioning for Tier 1 employers.

**Mahindra Finance:** Deepest semi-urban and rural reach (1,300+ branches). Branch-level sanction authority up to Rs 10L. Will approve profiles banks reject: 6-month employment, CIBIL 650 with guarantor, cash-salary with employer letter. 95% collection efficiency through weekly field collection. FOIR goes up to 65%.

**Cholamandalam (Chola):** Four product tiers within car loans — Banking, ITR, Cash Income, Income Program — each with its own rate, documentation, and risk appetite. Co-applicant mandatory for most products. Strong Maruti Suzuki tie-up. Rs 2.54L Cr AUM, 1,820 branches.

**SBI:** Lowest rates in the market (8.70% for CIBIL 800+, MCLR-linked). Zero foreclosure charges on floating-rate loans. YONO platform offers pre-approved instant approval for existing salary-account holders, but disbursement friction remains. Slowest TAT (7-10 days for non-pre-approved). Government and PSU employees get fastest processing.

**HDFC Bank:** Market leader, roughly 30% share of private-bank auto lending. Xpress Car Loan (30-minute disbursement) is the industry benchmark but only works when all conditions are met: existing HDFC customer, dealer empanelled, e-mandate active. Standard new-to-bank files take 24-48 hours. PADD model (Physical Approval + Digital Disbursement). Rs 1.3L Cr auto book.
