cercit · Credit Evaluation and Risk Compliance Intelligence Tool

An AI-assisted car loan appraisal system for banks and NBFCs, built during the Masai × IIT Roorkee PM programme. AI reads the documents and scores the risk, policy rules decide, and a credit officer signs off.

The timeline

Dates come from the git history and the project documents.

IdeationJul – 30 Aug

The idea and the PRD

A day-job pain (officers writing 25–40 credit appraisal memos a day) met the PM course. Three AI-assisted research passes were merged into one PRD.

  • Jul–AugMasai × IIT Roorkee PM course; picked salaried new-car loans as the problem
  • AugResearch from three angles: a 53-section solution blueprint, a 23-section technical spec, market research
  • 20 AugFirst commit: a Lovable prototype as the starting shell
  • 30 AugPRD v3.0: 20 open items closed; principle no. 1 is "Rules decide; AI assists"
Foundation29 Aug – 11 Sep

A working skeleton, live on the web

Database, decision engine and the first screens, deployed from day 12.

  • 30 AugSupabase backend wired end to end: submit, assess, review
  • 31 AugSite live on GitHub Pages
  • 2–3 SepSanction and approval letters; decision engine pipeline; row-level security
  • 8–9 SepPrototype rebuilt for production: 128 type errors fixed, demo mode
  • 10–11 SepCustomer landing page, loan application and status tracker
Credit engine16 – 24 Sep

Cloud, risk model and credit control

The parts a lender would actually trust: document reading, a risk model, and controlled policy changes.

  • 16 SepAWS document pipeline (Textract, Rekognition) and the XGBoost risk model; PAN and mobile encrypted
  • 17–19 SepPolicy changes go draft → impact test → approval → live; repayment history
  • 20 SepOne-command deploy for the AWS functions
  • 22 SepBank statement analysis with red flags
  • 24 SepUndeclared bureau loans send a case to a person; upload APIs locked to staff
Customer + polish25 Sep – 3 Oct

The customer journey and the details

The customer side and a long fix list.

  • 25–26 SepLender-grade landing page; investor and bank decks; roles and rights as data
  • 27 SepCustomer onboarding with document uploads
  • 29 SepAutomatic document checks and a fast lane for clean files
  • 1 OctTwo credit bureaus, the worse score counts
  • 2–3 OctFix-list sprint (81 commits in two days); daily simulation; how-it-works pack; real test documents erased
Audit4 – 8 Oct

An outside audit, checked line by line

Outside AI agents were asked to break it. About a third of their findings were wrong or already fixed. Five were real.

  • 4 OctAudit brief and report; every finding verified against code or the live database
Launch9 Oct

Launched: try it, break it

Everything a visitor needs to try it went in, along with the five audit fixes and the launch video.
