## Customer journey and documents

- **Step 4 pre-fill.** Step 4 keeps checking for document readings for about a minute, and has a "Fill from my documents" button that explains any error. Aadhaar addresses are cleaned of masked numbers and S/O or C/O prefixes, and the father's name comes from Aadhaar.
- **Aadhaar and PAN reader (AWS).** Downloaded e-Aadhaar and e-PAN PDFs are now read line by line, since they don't print "Label: value" pairs. Deployed 29 Sep.
- **Login fixes.** "8-digit code" wording, a clearer wrong-password message, and "Signups not allowed for otp" now explained in plain words. The email is trimmed and lowercased before sign-in.
- **Opening files.** "Open" on a staff document now opens a tab first, then loads the file, so it is no longer blocked as a pop-up. Errors show in that tab.
- **P1 closed (2 Oct).** The "AWS rejects the PDFs" fault was the test method, not AWS. All 7 of your documents read correctly.

## Automatic document checks (052)

- The rules are stored as data and editable on the **Document checks** page.
- Each document gets PASS, FAIL, UNREAD or WAITING.
- Clean files are auto-accepted, marked docs-verified, sent through the credit checks, and flagged **fast lane** in the queue.
- Readings from AWS are saved through one guarded database function (closes R20).

## Credit bureau detail (053)

- Two simulated bureaus per customer, each with accounts, a 24-month late-payment grid and enquiries. They are matched and combined worst-of into the single bureau row the engine already reads, so the engine and tests didn't change.
- Officers get a **Credit bureau** card.
- The engine no longer crashes on a customer neither bureau knows; it refers the file as missing data.
- Five policy points are built with the cautious option behind a switch each (to-do item 6).

## Income and bank detail (054)

- Salary slips, Form 16 Part B and a monthly bank summary for each file.
- Flags: hidden home loan, employer loan, loss-of-pay month, arrears, gaps between slips, irregular salary, bounces, low balance, slip/bank mismatch.
- Officers get an **Income** card.

## Synthetic customers and loans (055, 056)

- **055.** A generator that makes realistic synthetic customers end to end, with IDs `SYN…`, PANs with X as the 4th letter, mobiles starting 5550 and `@synthetic.invalid` emails. 2,000 are live: 772 approved, 603 referred, 322 declined, 191 submitted, 112 drafts. 055 also fixed the income check, which had compared take-home pay with Form 16 gross pay.
- **056.** 628 of the approved customers are disbursed 3–27 months back, with full repayment history: some bounces, some 31–60 day stretches, a few defaults.
